# XANORIDEL (xanoridostat) Prescribing Information, Tenant Pharma_A Confidential

## 2.1 Recommended Dosage
The recommended starting dose is **40 mg orally once daily**, with or without food.
- Measure serum uric acid 2 weeks after starting and after each dose change.
- Raise the dose by 40 mg every 2 weeks until the serum uric acid is below 6 mg/dL.

## 2.3 Renal Impairment Dosing
| Creatinine clearance | Starting dose | Maximum dose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 60 mL/min or more | 40 mg once daily | 160 mg once daily |
| 30 to 59 mL/min | 40 mg once daily | 80 mg once daily |
| 15 to 29 mL/min | 20 mg once daily | 40 mg once daily |
| Below 15 mL/min or on dialysis | Not recommended | Not recommended |

## 4 Contraindications
Xanoridel is contraindicated in patients:
- treated with azathioprine or mercaptopurine, because xanthine oxidase breaks these medicines down and their levels can rise to toxic levels;
- with a known allergy to xanoridostat or to any ingredient of the tablet;
- who had a serious skin reaction to Xanoridel or to another xanthine oxidase inhibitor.

## 5.1 Serious Skin Reactions
Stevens-Johnson syndrome, toxic epidermal necrolysis and drug reaction with eosinophilia and systemic symptoms were reported in 0.1% of patients, most in the first 8 weeks. Patients who carry the HLA-B*58:01 allele had a higher rate. Stop Xanoridel at the first rash and do not restart it.

## 8.1 Pregnancy
There are no human data on Xanoridel in pregnancy. In rats given 30 times the human exposure, there were more early losses of pregnancy; at 5 times the human exposure there was no effect. Use Xanoridel in pregnancy only if the benefit justifies the risk to the fetus. Report a pregnancy exposure to Pharma A Medical Safety.
